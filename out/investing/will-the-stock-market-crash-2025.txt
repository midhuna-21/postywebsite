The key is to integrate both defensive and opportunistic approaches, maintaining flexibility to adjust allocations in response to evolving market conditions while avoiding overreaction to short-term fluctuations. Education, research, and continuous monitoring are critical tools for making informed decisions, allowing investors to distinguish between temporary volatility and structural market risks.
