Donald Trump’s Stock Portfolio: 8 Stocks Owned by the President

  • by David Miller
  • October 10, 2025

A look at eight publicly disclosed stocks in President Donald Trump’s portfolio, including Trump Media, Apple, Microsoft, and Nvidia.

President Donald Trump with a net worth of approximately six billion dollars according to Forbes owns a diversified portfolio of stocks spanning media technology and finance His top holding is Trump Media and Technology Group Corp DJT which is the parent company of Truth Social valued at around two billion dollars This investment reflects a significant personal stake representing approximately forty one point five percent of the company and underscores the emphasis on media and technology platforms as part of his overall investment strategy Other notable positions in his portfolio include Blue Owl Capital Corp OBDC Apple Inc AAPL Microsoft Corp MSFT Nvidia Corp NVDA Blackstone Inc BX Broadcom Inc AVGO and Alphabet Inc GOOG GOOGL These holdings illustrate a blend of exposure to leading technology giants financial services firms and alternative investment vehicles with stakes ranging from hundreds of thousands to millions of dollars reflecting a strategy of diversification across multiple sectors Trump Media and Technology Group remains a cornerstone of President Trumps personal investments and indicates a focus on growth oriented media ventures that can leverage emerging social media and digital content trends Despite occasional public discussion about potentially selling shares Trump has stated that he has no immediate plans to divest from these holdings The composition of Trumps portfolio provides insight into the investment approach he favors which combines high profile technology companies with financial institutions and innovative media platforms His stakes in Apple Microsoft and Nvidia demonstrate a commitment to leading technology firms with strong market positions innovation capabilities and long term growth prospects These companies dominate key segments such as consumer electronics enterprise software cloud computing graphics processing and artificial intelligence AI technologies providing exposure to sectors expected to drive economic expansion in coming years Investments in Alphabet highlight a strategy that includes diversified digital advertising cloud computing and data driven business models while holdings in Broadcom emphasize semiconductors and networking technologies critical to modern infrastructure The inclusion of Blackstone and Blue Owl Capital indicates an interest in alternative investment vehicles private equity and credit markets that can offer returns through structured deals asset management fees and exposure to institutional investment trends Collectively these holdings reflect a portfolio designed to balance growth potential with exposure to financially resilient firms offering strategic market advantages Evaluating President Trumps portfolio provides perspective on sector allocation risk management and long term strategic positioning Media and technology investments represent significant weight in his portfolio indicating a preference for companies with scalable platforms high user engagement and potential for future monetization Financial sector investments provide stability income generation and diversification while participation in alternative investment firms allows for engagement with complex financial products and institutional strategies Disclosure filings submitted by President Trump and reviewed by regulatory authorities offer transparency into the structure size and nature of his holdings These disclosures are critical for understanding potential conflicts of interest ensuring public accountability and offering insight into investment decisions that may influence policy or business activities The size and scope of Trumps investments in technology and media suggest a recognition of the transformative impact of digital platforms AI cloud computing and social media on business models and economic trends Strategic allocations to companies such as Apple Microsoft Nvidia Broadcom and Alphabet position the portfolio to benefit from ongoing technological innovation high barriers to entry and global market penetration The emphasis on large cap technology firms also indicates a focus on companies with strong brand recognition established customer bases and significant intellectual property portfolios which can mitigate market volatility and provide long term growth stability The Trump Media and Technology Group holding emphasizes a high concentration personal investment strategy in a venture closely associated with Trumps brand identity media influence and potential revenue streams from advertising subscription services and content monetization This investment carries higher concentration risk compared with publicly traded diversified firms but reflects strategic positioning in a sector with significant growth opportunities Investors and analysts observe that the combination of diversified blue chip technology and finance holdings alongside concentrated media investments offers a unique perspective into risk tolerance return expectations and sector prioritization While diversification in the portfolio reduces idiosyncratic risk individual stakes in specific companies such as DJT demonstrate a willingness to maintain concentrated positions in ventures aligned with personal expertise brand and market vision Evaluating financial disclosures over time provides a longitudinal view of portfolio adjustments liquidity potential market influence and alignment with broader market trends The inclusion of alternative investment firms highlights engagement with complex financial structures and private market opportunities that complement traditional equity holdings The overall portfolio strategy suggests an emphasis on growth potential innovation adoption and market leadership while maintaining diversified exposure across multiple sectors and risk profiles Regulatory filings and public statements indicate that while some shares may be considered for future sale there is no immediate plan for divestment Maintaining long term positions in both technology and media reflects confidence in sector trajectories potential for capital appreciation and strategic market positioning The portfolio also demonstrates sensitivity to macroeconomic trends technological disruption and market leadership ensuring that investments are positioned to capitalize on secular growth drivers including AI
